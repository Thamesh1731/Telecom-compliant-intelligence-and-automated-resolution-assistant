    # PLN-05: Plan Expired or Benefits Stopped

## Category
Plans / Recharge

## Subcategory
Plan Expiry

## Problem
The customer reports that their mobile plan has expired, is about to
expire, or that plan benefits stopped being available after the expected
renewal or expiration date.

## Common Symptoms

- Mobile data stopped working after the plan expired
- Calls are no longer included
- SMS benefits are unavailable
- Customer's plan shows as expired
- Customer expected automatic renewal but it did not occur
- Customer recharged but the previous plan still shows as expired
- Customer received an expiration notification unexpectedly
- Plan benefits stopped before the customer expected them to

## Possible Causes

- Plan reached its expiration date
- Renewal payment failed
- AutoPay or Auto Refill was disabled
- Insufficient account balance for renewal
- Renewal transaction is still processing
- Plan benefits were exhausted before the expiration date
- Account or service restriction
- Renewal was applied to another line
- Provider-side renewal problem
- Plan was changed or cancelled
- Provider-specific plan rules

## Initial Diagnosis

Determine:

1. Current plan status.
2. Plan expiration date.
3. Whether the plan was expected to renew automatically.
4. Whether AutoPay or Auto Refill is enabled.
5. Whether sufficient funds were available for renewal.
6. Whether a renewal payment was attempted.
7. Payment/transaction status.
8. Current account balance.
9. Whether the customer recently changed plans.
10. Which specific benefits stopped working.

## Troubleshooting Procedure

### 1. Check Plan Status

Determine whether the plan is:

- Active
- Expired
- Pending renewal
- Scheduled for renewal
- Cancelled
- Suspended

### 2. Check Expiration Date

Verify the plan's actual expiration date using the provider's account
system.

Do not rely solely on the customer's memory or notification date.

### 3. Check Renewal Status

Determine whether automatic renewal was:

- Enabled
- Disabled
- Attempted
- Successful
- Failed
- Pending

### 4. Check Account Balance

For prepaid services, determine whether the account had sufficient
funds for the intended renewal.

### 5. Check Payment Status

If automatic or manual payment was used, determine whether the payment
was:

- Not attempted
- Declined
- Pending
- Successful
- Reversed

A failed renewal may originate from a payment problem rather than the
plan itself.

### 6. Check Plan Benefits

Determine whether all benefits stopped or only specific benefits.

Examples:

- Data stopped
- Calls stopped
- SMS stopped
- One plan feature stopped

If only one service is affected while the plan remains active, route
the complaint to the appropriate service troubleshooting workflow.

### 7. Check Account and Line

If multiple mobile lines exist, verify that the active plan and renewal
apply to the correct line.

## Diagnosis Guidance

### Plan expired on the expected date

If the plan reached its documented expiration date, explain the status
and provide the applicable renewal/recharge process.

### Plan expired unexpectedly

Check:

- Expiration date
- Plan history
- Recent plan changes
- Renewal status
- Account status

If the account records contradict the customer's expected expiration
date, escalate for investigation.

### Auto-renewal failed

Check:

- AutoPay/Auto Refill status
- Payment method
- Account balance
- Payment transaction
- Provider system status

If the payment failed, route through the payment/recharge workflow.

### Payment succeeded but plan expired

Do not tell the customer to pay again.

Verify:

- Payment
- Renewal transaction
- Plan status
- Correct mobile line

Escalate if the successful payment did not renew the plan.

### Plan is active but one benefit stopped

Do not classify this automatically as plan expiry.

Investigate the specific affected service, such as:

- Mobile data
- Voice calls
- SMS

## Resolution

If the plan legitimately expired, guide the customer through the
provider's available renewal or recharge process.

If automatic renewal failed because of a payment problem, resolve or
route the payment issue appropriately.

If the renewal succeeded but the plan remains expired, investigate the
account and escalate when necessary.

If the plan is active and only one benefit is unavailable, route the
complaint to the appropriate mobile-service workflow.

## Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:

- The plan status conflicts with the transaction history.
- A successful renewal payment did not reactivate the plan.
- Auto-renewal failed despite valid payment configuration.
- The expiration date appears incorrect.
- The wrong mobile line was renewed or allowed to expire.
- Account correction is required.
- Multiple customers report unexpected plan expiration.
- A provider-side renewal or account-system problem is suspected.
- Manual plan restoration is required.

## Agent Information to Record

Record:

- Ticket ID
- Customer complaint
- Plan name/identifier
- Plan status
- Expiration date
- Renewal date
- AutoPay/Auto Refill status
- Account balance
- Payment status
- Renewal transaction status
- Mobile line
- Benefits affected
- Recent plan changes
- Error messages
- Troubleshooting completed
- Action taken
- Escalation reason

Do not store unnecessary payment credentials.

## Agent Guidance

Do not assume that a stopped service means the entire plan expired.

First check the actual plan status.

Do not tell the customer to make another payment until any previous
renewal transaction has been verified.

Do not promise that a plan can be restored retroactively.

Do not promise retention of unused benefits, rollover data, promotional
pricing, or other plan features unless the applicable provider policy
confirms it.

Provider-specific expiration periods, grace periods, renewal rules,
plan benefits, and restoration policies should be maintained in
provider-specific policy documents.

## Example Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Plan Expired Normally

Customer:

"My prepaid plan expired today."

Action:

1. Verify plan status and expiration date.
2. Confirm the account status.
3. Provide the supported renewal/recharge process.

### Scenario 2: Auto-Renewal Failed

Customer:

"My plan was supposed to renew automatically but it expired."

Action:

1. Check AutoPay/Auto Refill.
2. Check payment status.
3. Check account balance.
4. Determine why renewal failed.
5. Route payment problems appropriately.

### Scenario 3: Payment Succeeded but Plan Expired

Customer:

"I paid for my renewal, but my phone still says my plan expired."

Action:

1. Verify payment.
2. Verify renewal transaction.
3. Verify plan status.
4. Verify mobile line.
5. Escalate if the successful renewal has not activated.

### Scenario 4: Data Stopped but Plan Is Active

Customer:

"My data stopped working, but my plan is still active."

Action:

Do not classify this as plan expiry.

Route the complaint to the mobile-data troubleshooting workflow.
