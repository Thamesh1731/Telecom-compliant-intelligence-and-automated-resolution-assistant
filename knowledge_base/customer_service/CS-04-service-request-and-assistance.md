# CS-04: Service Requests and Customer Assistance

## Category

customer_service

## Subcategory

Service Requests and Customer Assistance

## Problem

The customer wants assistance with a telecom service request that does not clearly correspond to a technical fault, billing issue, network problem, plan issue, security issue, or device problem.

The request may involve obtaining assistance, initiating a supported service request, understanding available procedures, or determining the correct process for completing a request.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "I want to request a change to my service."
- "How do I request this service?"
- "I need help with a service request."
- "Can customer support help me with this?"
- "I want to know how to make this request."
- "What is the process for requesting this?"
- "I need assistance with a telecom service request."

## AI Assessment

GENERAL SERVICE-REQUEST ASSISTANCE

The customer is requesting assistance with a telecom service process, but the complaint does not provide enough information to route it to a more specific technical, billing, account, plan, SIM, device, or network category.

## Recommended Human Action

Clarify the exact service or action the customer is requesting.

Determine:

- Which telecom service is involved.
- What action the customer wants performed.
- Whether the request affects an existing service.
- Whether identity or account verification is required.
- Whether the request requires a specialized department.
- Whether the request has eligibility or authorization requirements.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Identify the requested service or action.
2. Confirm the customer's account or service details when required.
3. Explain the applicable procedure.
4. Collect the information required to process the request.
5. Route the request to the appropriate department when necessary.
6. Record the request and relevant customer information.

## Routing Examples

Route to a more specific category when the request clearly concerns:

- Billing or charges → billing
- Plans or recharge → plans_recharge
- Network or coverage → coverage_outage
- Internet or data service → internet
- Mobile service → mobile_service
- Calling or number issues → number_calling
- SIM or eSIM → sim_esim
- Device problems → device_handset
- Account or subscription → account_subscription
- Security or suspected fraud → security_fraud
- Number porting → number_porting
- Cable television → cable_tv
- Technician or installation → installation_technician

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The requested action requires a specialist department.
- The request requires authorization not available to general support.
- The customer's eligibility cannot be determined.
- The request involves a complex account or service configuration.
- The standard service-request procedure cannot be completed.
- Previous attempts to process the request have failed.

## Important Notes

Do not assume the customer's intended service or request when the complaint is ambiguous.

Do not provide unsupported eligibility requirements, fees, processing times, or guarantees.

When sufficient information becomes available, route the request to the most specific applicable category.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom customer-support workflows for identifying and processing general service requests, collecting required information, routing requests to specialized departments, verifying applicable customer requirements, and escalating requests that cannot be completed through standard support.
