# CS-03: Account Support and Information Requests

## Category

customer_service

## Subcategory

Account Support and Information Requests

## Problem

The customer requires general assistance or information related to their telecom account but the request does not specifically concern billing, plan changes, SIM/eSIM problems, security, or another specialized category.

The customer may need help understanding account information, accessing account-related services, updating basic account details, or determining what information is required for a request.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "I need help with my account."
- "How can I update my account information?"
- "I need information about my account."
- "I cannot find some information in my account."
- "What information do I need to provide for this request?"
- "I need help accessing my customer account."
- "Can you explain what information is associated with my account?"

## AI Assessment

ACCOUNT SUPPORT OR INFORMATION REQUEST

The customer requires assistance related to account information or account support, but the request does not clearly indicate a specific technical, billing, security, subscription, or SIM-related problem.

## Recommended Human Action

Identify the exact account-related request and verify that the customer is authorized to receive or modify the requested information.

Determine:

- What account information the customer needs.
- Whether the customer wants to update information.
- Whether the request involves account access.
- Whether identity verification is required.
- Whether the request belongs to billing, subscription, security, or another specialized category.

## Account Information Handling

Only provide account-specific information after completing the applicable customer verification process.

Do not disclose:

- Personal account information to unauthorized parties.
- Account credentials.
- Authentication codes.
- Sensitive security information.
- Information belonging to another customer.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Clarify the customer's request.
2. Verify the customer when account-specific information is involved.
3. Determine the appropriate account-support procedure.
4. Provide the permitted information or assistance.
5. Route the request to a specialized category if necessary.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The customer cannot complete required account verification.
- The request involves sensitive account information.
- Account access cannot be restored through standard procedures.
- The request requires a specialized account team.
- The customer reports an account issue that may involve unauthorized access.
- The requested account change requires authorization beyond standard support.

## Important Notes

Do not assume that every account-related complaint belongs to the account category.

Requests involving payments or charges should be routed to billing.

Requests involving plans or subscriptions should be routed to the appropriate plan or subscription category.

Requests involving suspected unauthorized access should be routed to the security/fraud category.

Requests involving SIM or eSIM problems should be routed to the SIM/eSIM category.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom customer-support workflows for handling general account information requests, customer verification, account assistance, privacy protection, appropriate routing, and escalation of account-related issues.
