# CS-01: General Customer Support

## Category

customer_service

## Subcategory

General Customer Support

## Problem

The customer needs general assistance with a telecom service, account, product, or issue but the request does not clearly fall into a specific technical or billing category.

The customer may be asking for information, guidance, service assistance, or help understanding an existing issue.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "I need help with my mobile service."
- "Can someone help me with my account?"
- "I have a problem and I'm not sure which department handles it."
- "I need assistance with my telecom service."
- "I want to speak to customer support."
- "Can you help me with an issue on my account?"
- "I don't know who to contact about this problem."

## AI Assessment

GENERAL CUSTOMER-SERVICE REQUEST

The complaint does not contain sufficient specific information to confidently classify it into a technical, billing, network, roaming, plan, security, or device-related issue.

## Recommended Human Action

Identify the customer's primary issue and collect the relevant information before routing the request.

Confirm:

- Customer identity or authorized account access where required.
- The affected service.
- The customer's mobile number or relevant service identifier.
- A clear description of the problem.
- When the issue started.
- Whether the issue affects one service or multiple services.
- Any troubleshooting already performed.

Route the request to the appropriate specialist category once the issue is identified.

## Information to Collect

Ask the customer to provide:

- What service is affected?
- What exactly is not working?
- When did the problem begin?
- Is the problem constant or intermittent?
- Are other services working normally?
- Has anything changed recently?

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The customer's issue cannot be identified from the available information.
- The customer requires a specialist department.
- Multiple services are affected.
- The issue requires account-level investigation.
- The customer reports a repeated unresolved issue.
- Previous support attempts have not resolved the problem.

## Important Notes

Do not assume a technical cause when the complaint does not provide enough information.

Do not provide troubleshooting steps unrelated to the customer's stated problem.

The request should be classified into a more specific category whenever sufficient information becomes available.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom customer-support workflows for handling general service requests, identifying the customer's actual issue, collecting necessary information, routing requests to the appropriate support category, and escalating unresolved or specialist cases.
