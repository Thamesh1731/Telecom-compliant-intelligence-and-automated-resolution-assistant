# BILL-04: Refund or Account Credit

## Category
Billing

## Subcategory
Refund / Credit / Billing Adjustment

## Problem
The customer reports that they are owed a refund, account credit, or
billing adjustment, or they have an existing credit balance that they
want refunded.

## Common Scenarios

- Customer overpaid their bill
- Customer paid twice
- Customer was charged incorrectly
- Customer is owed a billing adjustment
- A previous billing dispute resulted in a credit
- Customer has a credit balance on an active account
- Customer has a remaining credit after cancelling service
- Customer is waiting for an approved refund

## Important Distinction

An account credit and a refund are not necessarily the same.

### Account Credit

A credit remains on the customer's telecom account and may be applied
toward a future bill.

### Refund

A refund returns eligible money to the customer through an applicable
refund method.

The exact treatment depends on the provider's current billing policy
and the customer's account status.

## Initial Diagnosis

Determine:

1. Why the customer is owed money.
2. Amount of the credit or expected refund.
3. Whether the credit has already been applied to the account.
4. Whether the customer's account is active or cancelled.
5. Whether the credit resulted from an overpayment, duplicate payment,
   billing correction, dispute, or another adjustment.
6. Whether the refund has already been initiated.
7. Whether the customer has received any refund confirmation.

## Troubleshooting Procedure

### Step 1: Verify the Credit

Check the account billing history and determine:

- Credit amount
- Date applied
- Reason for credit
- Billing period
- Whether the credit is still available

### Step 2: Determine Account Status

Determine whether the customer:

- Has active service
- Has cancelled service
- Is in the process of closing the account

An active account may use the credit toward a future bill rather than
receive an immediate refund, depending on provider policy.

### Step 3: Check Refund Eligibility

Determine whether the credit is eligible for a refund under the
applicable provider policy.

Do not assume that every account credit can automatically be refunded.

### Step 4: Check Refund Status

If a refund has already been requested, determine whether it is:

- Not requested
- Requested
- Processing
- Completed
- Failed
- Returned or undeliverable

### Step 5: Check Refund Method

If a refund is approved, verify the applicable refund method according
to the provider's policy.

Do not request sensitive payment information through an unsecured
support channel.

## Resolution

### Credit on Active Account

If the customer has an active account and the credit is valid, explain
how the credit will be handled according to the provider's policy.

If the customer is eligible to request a refund instead, initiate or
route the request through the applicable billing process.

### Approved Refund

If a refund has already been approved, provide the customer with its
current status and applicable processing information.

### Billing Correction Required

If the customer is owed money because of a billing error, submit the
appropriate billing adjustment before processing the refund when
required.

### Cancelled Account

If the account has been cancelled and a valid credit remains, follow the
provider's final-account and refund procedure.

## Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:

- The customer is eligible for a refund but the refund cannot be
  processed automatically.
- A confirmed credit is missing from the account.
- An approved refund has not been received within the applicable
  processing period.
- The refund failed or was returned.
- The credit amount is incorrect or disputed.
- A cancelled account still has an unresolved credit balance.
- The account contains conflicting billing information.
- Manual billing review is required.

## Agent Information to Record

Record:

- Ticket ID
- Credit/refund amount
- Reason for credit
- Date credit was applied
- Account status
- Refund eligibility
- Refund status
- Refund method, when applicable
- Previous refund attempts
- Customer's requested resolution
- Action taken
- Escalation reason, if applicable

## Agent Guidance

Do not promise a refund until eligibility has been verified.

Do not invent a refund timeframe.

Refund processing times and eligible refund methods vary by provider
and payment method.

If the customer has an active account with a valid credit, determine
whether the credit will automatically be applied to a future bill before
creating a separate refund request.

Do not expose or request unnecessary payment information.

## Example Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Overpayment

Customer paid more than the amount due.

Expected handling:

1. Verify the payment.
2. Confirm the resulting credit.
3. Determine whether the credit remains on the account.
4. Apply the provider's credit/refund policy.

### Scenario 2: Duplicate Payment

Customer accidentally paid the same bill twice.

Expected handling:

1. Verify both payments.
2. Confirm the resulting account credit.
3. Determine refund eligibility.
4. Process or escalate the refund request.

### Scenario 3: Billing Error

Customer was incorrectly charged and the provider confirms the error.

Expected handling:

1. Correct the billing error.
2. Apply the appropriate credit or adjustment.
3. Determine whether the customer is eligible for a refund.

### Scenario 4: Refund Already Requested

Customer says a refund was approved but has not arrived.

Expected handling:

1. Verify the refund request.
2. Check its current status.
3. Check whether the applicable processing period has elapsed.
4. Escalate if the refund is overdue or failed.
