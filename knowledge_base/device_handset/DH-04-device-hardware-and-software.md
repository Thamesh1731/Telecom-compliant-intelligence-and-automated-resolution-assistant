# DH-04: Device Hardware and Software Issues

## Category

device_handset

## Subcategory

Device Hardware and Software Issues

## Problem

The customer reports a specific hardware or software problem affecting the normal operation of a mobile device.

The issue may involve the screen, battery, charging, camera, speakers, microphone, buttons, operating system, applications, or other device functions.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My phone is not charging properly."
- "The screen is not responding."
- "My phone battery drains very quickly."
- "The phone keeps overheating."
- "My camera is not working."
- "The speaker has stopped working."
- "My microphone does not work."
- "An app keeps crashing on my phone."
- "My phone has a software problem."
- "My phone's screen is flickering."

## AI Assessment

POTENTIAL DEVICE HARDWARE OR SOFTWARE ISSUE

The complaint indicates a specific handset hardware, operating-system, or application-related problem.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine whether the issue is related to:

- Display or touchscreen.
- Battery or power.
- Charging.
- Camera.
- Speaker or audio.
- Microphone.
- Physical buttons.
- Operating system.
- Applications.
- Device performance.
- Overheating.

Check:

- When the problem started.
- Whether the issue is constant or intermittent.
- Whether the device was recently updated.
- Whether the device has physical damage.
- Whether the issue began after installing an application.
- Whether basic device functions remain available.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Where appropriate:

1. Restart the device.
2. Check whether system software is up to date.
3. Check the charging cable, adapter, and charging port for obvious external issues.
4. Close and reopen affected applications.
5. Check available device storage.
6. Test the affected function again.
7. Record any error message displayed.

For overheating, stop intensive use and allow the device to cool before further troubleshooting.

Do not instruct the customer to open or physically repair the device.

## Diagnostic Interpretation

If only one application is affected, investigate the application or software configuration.

If multiple device functions are affected, investigate the operating system or hardware.

If the device has visible physical or liquid damage, hardware assessment may be required.

If charging fails across compatible chargers and the device remains unresponsive, investigate a potential hardware or battery issue.

If the issue began after a software update, investigate software compatibility or configuration before assuming hardware failure.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Identify the affected device component or software function.
2. Determine whether the issue is hardware or software related.
3. Check available device diagnostics.
4. Guide the customer through approved troubleshooting.
5. Determine whether authorized repair or replacement is required.
6. Route service-related issues to the appropriate telecom category.

## Routing

Route to `mobile_service` when the handset itself functions normally but the mobile service is unavailable.

Route to `sim_esim` when the problem specifically concerns SIM or eSIM detection, activation, or replacement.

Route to `number_calling` when the primary issue concerns calling or number functionality.

Route to `internet` when the primary issue concerns internet service rather than the handset itself.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Hardware failure is suspected.
- The device has physical or liquid damage.
- The device remains unusable after approved troubleshooting.
- A battery or charging fault is suspected.
- The device requires authorized repair or replacement.
- A persistent operating-system problem cannot be resolved remotely.

## Important Notes

Do not assume a hardware fault without appropriate evidence.

Do not instruct customers to open, modify, or physically repair the device.

Do not recommend unofficial firmware, rooting, or unsupported repair procedures.

Do not promise warranty coverage, replacement, repair costs, or turnaround times without confirmation.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom device-support workflows for diagnosing handset hardware and software problems, distinguishing application and operating-system issues from hardware faults, performing safe troubleshooting, routing service-related complaints appropriately, and escalating cases requiring authorized repair or replacement.
