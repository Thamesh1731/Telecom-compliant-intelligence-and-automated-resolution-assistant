# DH-01: Mobile Device Not Working

## Category

device_handset

## Subcategory

Device Not Working

## Problem

The customer reports that their mobile phone or handset is not functioning normally.

The issue may involve the device failing to power on, freezing, restarting unexpectedly, becoming unresponsive, or experiencing a general malfunction.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My phone is not working."
- "My phone won't turn on."
- "My phone is frozen."
- "My phone keeps restarting."
- "My handset stopped working."
- "My phone is completely unresponsive."
- "The phone suddenly stopped working."
- "My device keeps crashing."

## AI Assessment

POTENTIAL MOBILE DEVICE ISSUE

The complaint indicates a possible handset hardware, software, power, or system problem.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine:

- Whether the device powers on.
- Whether the screen responds.
- Whether the device is frozen or restarting.
- Whether the problem started after an update, application installation, drop, liquid exposure, or other event.
- Whether the SIM is detected.
- Whether charging works.
- Whether mobile services are affected or only the device itself.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1. Check whether the device has sufficient battery charge.
2. Connect the device to a compatible charger.
3. Allow the device to charge for an appropriate period.
4. Restart the device if it responds.
5. If the device is frozen, use the manufacturer's approved force-restart procedure.
6. Check whether the device displays any error message.
7. Test basic device functions after restarting.

## Diagnostic Interpretation

If the device does not power on despite charging, investigate a possible battery, charger, or hardware issue.

If the device powers on but repeatedly freezes or restarts, investigate software or hardware causes.

If the device works normally but mobile services do not, route the issue to the appropriate SIM, network, calling, or mobile-service category.

If the problem began immediately after a software update or application installation, investigate possible software compatibility or configuration issues.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Identify the device model where relevant.
2. Determine the exact device behavior.
3. Establish whether the problem affects the entire device or a specific service.
4. Guide the customer through approved basic troubleshooting.
5. Check available warranty or authorized repair options where applicable.
6. Escalate unresolved hardware or software problems.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The device remains completely unresponsive.
- The device repeatedly crashes or restarts.
- A hardware failure is suspected.
- The device has physical damage affecting operation.
- The device has suffered liquid exposure.
- Standard troubleshooting does not resolve the issue.
- Authorized repair or replacement is required.

## Important Notes

Do not assume that a device problem is responsible for every mobile-service issue.

Do not instruct customers to open or physically repair the device.

Do not recommend unsupported firmware modifications or unofficial repair procedures.

Do not promise repair, replacement, warranty coverage, or costs without confirming the applicable provider or manufacturer policy.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom device-support workflows for diagnosing general handset failures, distinguishing device problems from mobile-service issues, performing safe basic troubleshooting, identifying potential hardware or software faults, and escalating cases requiring authorized repair or replacement.
