# TV-01: Cable TV Channel and Service Issues

## Category

cable_tv

## Subcategory

Cable TV Channel and Service Issues

## Problem

The customer reports that their cable television service is unavailable or that one or more television channels are not working as expected.

The issue may affect the entire TV service, specific channels, or access to subscribed programming.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My cable TV is not working."
- "I cannot watch any channels."
- "Some channels are not working."
- "My TV channels have disappeared."
- "I can only watch some of my channels."
- "The cable TV service stopped working."
- "I am unable to access my TV channels."
- "Several channels are unavailable."

## AI Assessment

CABLE TV SERVICE OR CHANNEL AVAILABILITY ISSUE

The customer is reporting a problem with cable television service or channel availability.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine whether the problem affects:

- All channels.
- Specific channels.
- Premium or subscribed channels.
- Recently added channels.
- Live television service.
- On-demand television service.

Check:

- Cable TV service status.
- Account and subscription status.
- Channel authorization.
- Known service outages.
- Cable connection.
- TV equipment status.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1. Confirm that the TV and cable equipment are powered on.
2. Verify that the correct TV input/source is selected.
3. Check cable connections between the wall outlet and TV equipment.
4. Restart the applicable cable TV equipment.
5. Wait for the equipment to reconnect.
6. Test multiple channels.
7. Check whether other subscribed services are working.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Identify whether the issue affects all or only some channels.
2. Verify the customer's cable TV subscription.
3. Check for known service interruptions.
4. Verify channel authorization where applicable.
5. Guide the customer through basic troubleshooting.
6. Escalate to technical support when the issue cannot be resolved remotely.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- All channels remain unavailable after basic troubleshooting.
- Multiple customers or locations appear affected.
- The issue indicates a possible network or service outage.
- Cable equipment does not reconnect.
- The customer's service is active but channels remain unauthorized.
- A physical line or installation issue is suspected.
- The problem persists after standard troubleshooting.

## Important Notes

Do not assume that a missing channel indicates a network outage.

A single unavailable channel may indicate channel authorization, programming, or service-specific issues rather than a complete cable TV failure.

Do not promise restoration times unless an authorized outage or service estimate is available.
