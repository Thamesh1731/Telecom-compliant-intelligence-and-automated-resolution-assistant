# TV-02: Cable TV Signal and Picture Quality

## Category

cable_tv

## Subcategory

Signal and Picture Quality

## Problem

The customer reports poor cable television picture or audio quality, including pixelation, freezing, distortion, missing audio, intermittent service, or unstable television reception.

The issue may affect one channel, multiple channels, or the entire cable TV service.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My cable TV picture keeps freezing."
- "The channels are pixelated."
- "The TV picture is breaking up."
- "My cable channels have poor picture quality."
- "The picture keeps disappearing."
- "My TV signal is unstable."
- "The picture is distorted."
- "I have picture but no sound."
- "The TV keeps freezing and coming back."

## AI Assessment

CABLE TV SIGNAL OR QUALITY ISSUE

The complaint indicates degraded television service quality rather than complete loss of service.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine:

- Whether the issue affects one channel or multiple channels.
- Whether the issue is continuous or intermittent.
- Whether picture, audio, or both are affected.
- Whether the issue occurs at specific times.
- Whether other cable TV services are affected.

Check:

- Cable connections.
- Cable TV equipment status.
- Known service interruptions.
- Signal quality where available.
- Whether the problem occurs on multiple channels.
- Whether other equipment connected to the service has the same problem.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1. Check that the cable connections are firmly connected.
2. Restart the cable TV receiver or set-top box.
3. Allow the equipment to fully reconnect.
4. Test multiple channels.
5. Check whether the issue occurs on another television connected to the same service, when applicable.
6. Verify that the TV is using the correct input/source.
7. Check whether both picture and audio are affected.

## Diagnostic Interpretation

If only one channel is affected, investigate channel-specific service or authorization issues.

If multiple channels are affected, investigate the cable signal, equipment, or network connection.

If the problem occurs intermittently, investigate signal instability or possible service interruptions.

If picture and audio are both lost simultaneously, investigate the service connection and cable equipment.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Identify the affected channels.
2. Determine the type of quality problem.
3. Check for known outages or service incidents.
4. Guide the customer through basic equipment and connection checks.
5. Check available signal information.
6. Escalate to technical support if the issue persists.

## Escalation

Escalate when:


- The issue persists after equipment restart and connection checks.
- Signal quality is abnormal.
- A physical cable or infrastructure problem is suspected.
- Multiple services at the same location are affected.

## Important Notes

Do not assume that every picture-quality issue is caused by the customer's television.

Do not replace or reset equipment unnecessarily before basic diagnostics are completed.

Do not promise a specific restoration time unless an authorized service estimate is available.
