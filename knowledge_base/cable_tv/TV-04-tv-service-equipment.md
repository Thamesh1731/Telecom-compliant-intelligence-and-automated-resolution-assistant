# TV-04: Cable TV Service Equipment

## Category

cable_tv

## Subcategory

TV Service Equipment

## Problem

The customer reports a problem with equipment used to access cable television services, such as a set-top box, cable receiver, remote control, or related TV service equipment.

The equipment may fail to power on, respond correctly, connect to the cable service, or provide normal television functionality.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My cable box is not working."
- "My set-top box won't turn on."
- "The TV receiver is stuck."
- "My cable box keeps restarting."
- "The remote is not working with my cable box."
- "My cable receiver is not connecting."
- "The cable box shows an error."
- "My TV equipment stopped working."

## AI Assessment

CABLE TV EQUIPMENT ISSUE

The complaint indicates a potential problem with cable television service equipment rather than the customer's television subscription itself.

## Recommended Human Action

Identify the affected equipment:

- Set-top box.
- Cable receiver.
- Remote control.
- Power adapter.
- Cable connection.
- Other provider-supplied TV equipment.

Determine:

- Whether the equipment powers on.
- Whether indicator lights are active.
- Whether an error message is displayed.
- Whether the equipment completes startup.
- Whether the equipment is connected to the cable network.
- Whether the problem affects one device or multiple devices.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1. Confirm that the equipment is connected to power.
2. Check that the power cable is securely connected.
3. Verify the TV is set to the correct input/source.
4. Check the cable connection between the equipment and wall outlet.
5. Restart the cable TV equipment.
6. Allow the equipment to complete its startup process.
7. Test the remote control and replace batteries if appropriate.
8. Record any error message displayed by the equipment.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Identify the affected equipment.
2. Confirm the equipment status.
3. Check for known equipment or service incidents.
4. Guide the customer through approved troubleshooting.
5. Determine whether remote diagnostics are available.
6. Arrange equipment replacement or technician support when authorized.
7. Escalate unresolved equipment faults.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Equipment does not power on after basic checks.
- Equipment repeatedly restarts or fails during startup.
- The equipment displays a persistent error.
- Remote diagnostics identify a hardware or connection fault.
- Physical equipment replacement is required.
- A technician visit is required.
- The problem persists after standard troubleshooting.

## Important Notes

Do not instruct customers to open, modify, or physically repair provider-supplied equipment.

Do not assume that equipment failure is the cause of a service outage without appropriate diagnostics.

Do not promise equipment replacement or technician availability without confirming the applicable process.
