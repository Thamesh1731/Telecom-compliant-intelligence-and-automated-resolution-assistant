# IT-02: Technician Visit and Appointment

## Category

installation_technician

## Subcategory

Technician Visit and Appointment

## Problem

The customer needs information about, wants to schedule, reschedule, or prepare for a technician visit related to installation, service setup, repair, or physical telecom infrastructure.

The request may concern an existing technician appointment or the need for an on-site visit.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "I need a technician to visit my home."
- "When is the technician coming?"
- "I want to schedule a technician visit."
- "Can I change my technician appointment?"
- "I need to reschedule the installation."
- "The technician was supposed to come but hasn't arrived."
- "What do I need to prepare before the technician arrives?"
- "I missed my technician appointment."

## AI Assessment

TECHNICIAN VISIT OR APPOINTMENT REQUEST

The customer requires an on-site technician visit or assistance related to an existing technician appointment.

## Recommended Human Action

Check:

- Existing service or installation order.
- Technician appointment status.
- Scheduled date and time.
- Service address.
- Technician assignment where available.
- Appointment requirements.
- Any previous visit notes.
- Whether the visit requires customer presence.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Verify the customer's account where required.
2. Locate the relevant service or installation order.
3. Check the current appointment status.
4. Confirm the service address.
5. Confirm or arrange the applicable appointment.
6. Explain any required access or preparation.
7. Provide the applicable appointment or request reference.

## Rescheduling

If the customer wants to reschedule:

- Confirm the existing appointment.
- Check available appointment slots.
- Select an authorized alternative.
- Confirm the new appointment details.
- Record the change.

Do not promise a specific appointment time without checking availability.

## Missed Appointment

If the technician did not arrive:

1. Check the appointment status.
2. Verify whether the appointment was cancelled or rescheduled.
3. Review available technician notes.
4. Determine whether a new appointment is required.
5. Escalate when the appointment failure cannot be resolved through standard procedures.

## Preparation

Where applicable, advise the customer to:

- Ensure access to the installation or equipment location.
- Keep required equipment available.
- Ensure an authorized person is present if required.
- Provide safe access to relevant equipment or service areas.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The technician repeatedly fails to attend.
- The appointment status is incorrect.
- The technician cannot complete the required work.
- A site assessment is required.
- The installation or repair requires specialist equipment.
- The appointment cannot be scheduled through the standard system.
- The customer has experienced repeated failed visits.

## Important Notes

Do not guarantee technician arrival times unless confirmed by the scheduling system.

Do not provide unsupported appointment availability.

Do not instruct customers to perform electrical, cable, or infrastructure work that requires a qualified technician.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom technician-support workflows for scheduling and managing installation or service visits, verifying appointment status, handling rescheduling and missed appointments, preparing customers for technician visits, and escalating failed or complex on-site service requests.
