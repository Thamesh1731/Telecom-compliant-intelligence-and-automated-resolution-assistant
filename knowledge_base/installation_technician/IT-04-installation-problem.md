# IT-04: Installation Problem and Failed Installation

## Category

installation_technician

## Subcategory

Installation Problem and Failed Installation

## Problem

The customer reports that a scheduled installation was attempted but could not be completed, or that the installation was completed incorrectly and the service is not functioning as expected.

The issue may involve incomplete installation, incorrect equipment setup, physical connection problems, site access issues, or infrastructure limitations.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "The technician installed the service but it is not working."
- "My installation could not be completed."
- "The technician said there was a problem with the installation."
- "The installation was completed but I still have no service."
- "The technician could not connect my service."
- "My new connection is not working after installation."
- "The installation failed."
- "The technician left without completing the installation."

## AI Assessment

FAILED OR INCOMPLETE SERVICE INSTALLATION

The customer reports that the installation process was unsuccessful, incomplete, or did not result in a functioning service.

## Recommended Human Action

Check:

- Installation order status.
- Technician visit notes.
- Installation completion status.
- Service activation status.
- Equipment installation status.
- Physical connection status.
- Infrastructure availability.
- Any reported site or access restrictions.
- Whether additional technician work is required.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Where appropriate, ask the customer to:

1. Confirm that installed equipment has power.
2. Check that provider equipment is connected as instructed.
3. Check the equipment status indicators.
4. Restart the installed equipment if authorized.
5. Confirm whether the service was ever working after installation.
6. Record any displayed error messages.

Do not instruct the customer to modify physical infrastructure or perform work requiring a qualified technician.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Verify the installation order.
2. Review technician notes.
3. Determine what part of the installation failed.
4. Check whether the service has been activated.
5. Check for infrastructure or equipment issues.
6. Arrange a follow-up technician visit when required.
7. Escalate unresolved installation failures to the appropriate technical team.

## Common Failure Conditions

Possible causes may include:

- Service infrastructure unavailable at the location.
- Required equipment unavailable.
- Incorrect or incomplete physical installation.
- Service activation not completed.
- Damaged or unsuitable connection.
- Site access limitations.
- Additional infrastructure work required.
- Equipment configuration failure.

These are possibilities to investigate, not confirmed causes.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The installation cannot be completed remotely.
- Physical infrastructure work is required.
- Service activation remains incomplete.
- The technician reports an infrastructure limitation.
- Equipment requires replacement or specialist configuration.
- A follow-up site visit is required.
- The installation has failed repeatedly.

## Important Notes

Do not assume the exact cause of an installation failure without technician or system evidence.

Do not instruct customers to perform hazardous electrical or infrastructure work.

Do not promise that a follow-up installation will succeed without confirming the underlying issue.

Do not provide unsupported technician schedules or restoration times.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom installation-support workflows for investigating incomplete or failed installations, reviewing technician findings, verifying service activation, identifying equipment and infrastructure dependencies, arranging follow-up visits, and escalating installation failures requiring specialist intervention.
