# NET-03: Intermittent Internet Connection

## Category
Internet / Network

## Problem
The customer reports that the internet connection repeatedly disconnects, becomes unstable, or stops working temporarily and then reconnects.

## Common Symptoms
- Internet disconnects and reconnects repeatedly
- Wi-Fi connection drops intermittently
- Online calls or games disconnect
- Websites periodically stop loading
- Connection becomes unstable at certain locations or times

## Possible Causes
- Weak Wi-Fi signal
- Wireless interference
- Router or gateway instability
- Network congestion
- Equipment or cable problems
- Service outage
- Network-side instability

## Troubleshooting Procedure

1. Determine whether the issue affects one device or multiple devices.
2. Check whether the issue occurs only in a particular location within the home.
3. Check router or gateway status indicators.
4. Check physical power and network connections.
5. Move the affected device closer to the router and test again.
6. Reduce unnecessary connected-device activity.
7. Restart the router or gateway.
8. Check for a known service outage.
9. If possible, test using a wired connection.
10. If the problem continues across devices and connection types, investigate a possible network-side issue.

## Resolution

If the instability is caused by local Wi-Fi conditions, equipment, or device-specific factors, provide the appropriate troubleshooting steps.

If the connection continues to drop after standard troubleshooting, escalate the issue for technical investigation.

## Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:

- Repeated disconnections continue after troubleshooting.
- Multiple devices are affected.
- A wired connection also experiences interruptions.
- A service outage or network-side fault is suspected.
- The customer has repeatedly reported the same unresolved issue.

## Agent Guidance

Record whether the issue affects one device, multiple devices, Wi-Fi only, or both wired and wireless connections. This information helps technical support distinguish local Wi-Fi problems from broader network problems.
