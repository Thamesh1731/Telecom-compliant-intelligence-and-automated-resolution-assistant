# NET-01: No Internet Connection

## Category
Internet / Network

## Problem
The customer reports that their internet service is unavailable or they cannot access the internet.

## Common Symptoms
- No internet access on one or more devices
- Multiple devices cannot connect
- Router or gateway indicates a connection problem
- Websites and online services cannot be reached
- Connection was previously working but has stopped

## Possible Causes
- Local router or gateway issue
- Loose or disconnected network cable
- Internet service outage
- Equipment failure
- Device-specific connectivity problem
- Network-side problem

## Troubleshooting Procedure

1. Check whether there is a known internet service outage affecting the customer's area.
2. Check that the router or gateway has power.
3. Check that the network cables and power connections are securely connected.
4. Check the router or gateway status indicators for connection problems.
5. Restart the router or gateway.
6. Wait for the equipment to complete its startup process.
7. Try connecting to the internet again.
8. If possible, test another device to determine whether the issue affects one device or multiple devices.
9. If multiple devices remain unable to connect, treat the issue as a possible network or service-level problem.

## Resolution

If the connection is restored after troubleshooting, confirm that internet access has returned.

If the connection remains unavailable after standard troubleshooting, the complaint should be investigated by technical support.

## Escalation Conditions

Escalate the complaint when: 

- The internet remains unavailable after standard troubleshooting.
- Router or gateway indicators show a persistent connection problem. 
- Multiple devices are affected. 
- The same issue has been repeatedly reported. 
- Equipment appears to be malfunctioning. 

## Agent Guidance

Do not repeatedly recommend restarting equipment if the customer has already completed the standard troubleshooting steps.

If the issue cannot be resolved using the available troubleshooting procedures, provide the customer with the escalation status and forward the case to the appropriate technical support team.
