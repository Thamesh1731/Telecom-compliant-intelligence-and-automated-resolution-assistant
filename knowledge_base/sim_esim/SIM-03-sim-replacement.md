# SIM-03: SIM Card Replacement

## Category

sim_esim

## Subcategory

SIM Card Replacement

## Problem

The customer needs a replacement physical SIM because the existing SIM is damaged, lost, defective, expired, or otherwise unusable.

The request may also involve transferring an existing mobile number or service to a replacement SIM.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "I need a replacement SIM."
- "I lost my SIM card."
- "My SIM is damaged."
- "My SIM stopped working and I need a new one."
- "How can I replace my SIM?"
- "I need a new SIM with the same number."
- "My SIM is broken."
- "Can I get a replacement SIM?"

## AI Assessment

SIM REPLACEMENT REQUEST

The customer requires a new physical SIM to replace an existing SIM while maintaining the applicable mobile service.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine:

- Reason for replacement.
- Whether the existing SIM is available.
- Whether the SIM is lost, damaged, or defective.
- Mobile number associated with the SIM.
- Customer account status.
- Whether identity verification is required.
- Whether the replacement process is supported through the available channel.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Verify the customer's identity using the authorized process.
2. Confirm the affected mobile line.
3. Determine the reason for replacement.
4. Check the account and SIM status.
5. Initiate the approved SIM replacement process.
6. Provide applicable activation or setup instructions.
7. Confirm the status of the replacement where available.

## Lost or Stolen SIM

If the customer reports that the SIM is lost or stolen:

- Follow the provider's authorized procedure for securing the mobile line.
- Verify the customer's identity.
- Determine whether the existing SIM should be deactivated.
- Initiate replacement through the approved process.

Do not disclose or provide sensitive authentication information.

## Damaged or Defective SIM

If the SIM is damaged or defective:

- Confirm whether the device detects the SIM.
- Determine whether the SIM works in another compatible device when appropriate.
- Check the mobile-line status.
- Initiate replacement when the SIM is confirmed defective or unusable.

## Important Checks

Before replacing a SIM, verify:

- Correct customer account.
- Correct mobile number.
- Customer authorization.
- Current line status.
- Existing SIM status where applicable.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Customer verification cannot be completed.
- The mobile line cannot be identified.
- The replacement request cannot be processed.
- The account contains conflicting information.
- The replacement requires specialist authorization.
- The customer's number or service cannot be transferred to the replacement SIM through the standard process.

## Important Notes

Do not provide replacement SIM credentials, activation codes, or authentication information.

Do not deactivate an active SIM without completing the required authorization and security process.

Do not promise replacement delivery times, fees, or eligibility unless confirmed by the applicable provider system or policy.

## Source Basis

This knowledge article is based on standardized telecom support workflows for replacing lost, damaged, or defective SIM cards, verifying customer identity, protecting mobile-line access, maintaining service continuity, and escalating SIM replacement cases requiring specialist intervention.
