# SIM-04: SIM Detection and Connectivity

## Category

sim_esim

## Subcategory

SIM Detection and Connectivity

## Problem

The customer reports that the device recognizes the SIM but the mobile line does not connect to the network correctly.

The issue may involve network registration, SIM provisioning, mobile-line activation, or connectivity after the SIM has been detected.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My phone detects the SIM but there is no network."
- "The SIM is showing, but I have no service."
- "My SIM is recognized but I cannot connect."
- "The SIM works but there is no signal."
- "My phone shows the SIM but says no service."
- "The SIM is detected but mobile data does not work."
- "My SIM is active but the phone won't connect to the network."
- "I have a SIM inserted but there is no mobile service."

## AI Assessment

POTENTIAL SIM REGISTRATION OR MOBILE-LINE CONNECTIVITY ISSUE

The device appears to recognize the SIM, but the mobile line is not successfully connecting to the network.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine whether the issue affects:

- Network registration.
- Voice calls.
- SMS.
- Mobile data.
- All mobile services.

Check:

- SIM detection status.
- Mobile-line activation status.
- Network registration.
- Device network settings.
- Airplane Mode status.
- SIM/eSIM enablement.
- Account or line provisioning.
- Coverage at the customer's location.
- Known network incidents.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1. Confirm that Airplane Mode is disabled.
2. Confirm that the SIM is enabled.
3. Restart the device.
4. Check whether the device displays the correct mobile network.
5. Move to an area with stronger coverage.
6. Test the SIM again after reconnecting to the network.
7. Verify that mobile data and calling are enabled where applicable.

## Diagnostic Interpretation

If the SIM is detected but no network is available, investigate network registration, coverage, account status, or line provisioning.

If the SIM works normally in another compatible device, investigate the original device or its network configuration.

If multiple devices cannot register the same SIM, investigate the SIM or mobile-line status.

If only one service such as calls or data is affected, route the complaint to the most specific applicable service category.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Verify the customer's account and mobile line where required.
2. Confirm that the SIM is detected.
3. Check the mobile-line activation status.
4. Check network registration information.
5. Review available coverage or outage information.
6. Guide the customer through approved troubleshooting.
7. Escalate provisioning or persistent registration issues.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The SIM remains unable to register after standard troubleshooting.
- The line appears active but cannot connect to the network.
- Provisioning information is inconsistent.
- The issue persists across compatible devices.
- A network-side investigation is required.
- SIM replacement or re-provisioning may be necessary.

## Important Notes

Do not assume that no service is caused by a defective SIM.

Coverage and network outages can produce symptoms similar to SIM connectivity problems.

Do not modify network or account provisioning without authorization.

Do not promise a specific restoration time unless confirmed by an authorized system.
