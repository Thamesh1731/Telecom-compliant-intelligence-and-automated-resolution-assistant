# SIM-01: SIM Card Not Working

## Category

sim_esim

## Subcategory

SIM Card Not Working

## Problem

The customer reports that their physical SIM card is not working correctly or that the device cannot use mobile services through the SIM.

The issue may affect network registration, calls, SMS, mobile data, or the device's ability to recognize the SIM.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My SIM card is not working."
- "My phone says no SIM."
- "The SIM is not detected."
- "My SIM suddenly stopped working."
- "My phone cannot read the SIM card."
- "The SIM works in another phone but not mine."
- "I inserted my SIM but there is no network."
- "My SIM card is showing an error."

## AI Assessment

POTENTIAL SIM CARD OR SIM REGISTRATION ISSUE

The complaint indicates that the physical SIM may not be detected, recognized, or properly registered for mobile service.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine whether the problem concerns:

- SIM detection.
- Network registration.
- Calling.
- SMS.
- Mobile data.
- SIM-related error messages.

Check:

- Whether the SIM is physically inserted correctly.
- Whether the device recognizes the SIM.
- Whether the SIM is enabled.
- Whether the mobile line is active.
- Whether the issue occurs in another compatible device.
- Whether there is a known network or account issue.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1. Turn off the device.
2. Remove and carefully reinsert the SIM.
3. Confirm that the SIM is positioned correctly.
4. Restart the device.
5. Check whether the SIM is detected.
6. Verify that the mobile line is enabled.
7. Test the SIM in another compatible device when appropriate.

Do not instruct the customer to physically modify or damage the SIM.

## Diagnostic Interpretation

If the SIM is not detected in any compatible device, the SIM may be damaged or defective.

If the SIM works in another device, investigate the original device or its SIM tray.

If the SIM is detected but has no network service, investigate network registration, account status, coverage, or service provisioning.

If only calls, SMS, or data are affected, route the issue to the most specific applicable service category when sufficient information is available.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Identify the affected SIM and mobile line.
2. Verify the customer's account where required.
3. Determine whether the SIM is detected.
4. Check the line and service status.
5. Guide the customer through approved troubleshooting.
6. Determine whether SIM replacement or further investigation is required.
7. Escalate persistent issues.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The SIM is not detected after troubleshooting.
- The SIM appears defective.
- The mobile line cannot be provisioned correctly.
- SIM replacement is required.
- The issue persists across compatible devices.
- Account or network provisioning requires specialist investigation.

## Important Notes

Do not assume that every "no network" complaint is caused by the SIM.

A SIM that is detected by the device may still have a network, account, or provisioning problem.

Do not provide replacement procedures, fees, or eligibility requirements unless confirmed by the applicable provider process.
