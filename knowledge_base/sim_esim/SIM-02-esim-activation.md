# SIM-02: eSIM Activation

## Category

sim_esim

## Subcategory

eSIM Activation and Setup

## Problem

The customer is unable to activate, install, or use an eSIM on a compatible device.

The issue may occur during eSIM installation, activation, QR-code scanning, profile download, or network registration after activation.

## Example Customer Complaints

- "My eSIM won't activate."
- "I can't install my eSIM."
- "The eSIM QR code isn't working."
- "My phone won't download the eSIM."
- "The eSIM installed but I have no network."
- "I cannot activate my eSIM."
- "My eSIM profile is not downloading."
- "My new eSIM isn't working."

## AI Assessment

POTENTIAL eSIM ACTIVATION OR PROFILE INSTALLATION ISSUE

The complaint indicates a problem with eSIM setup, profile installation, activation, or subsequent network registration.

## Recommended Human Action

Determine whether the problem occurs during:

- QR-code scanning.
- eSIM profile download.
- eSIM installation.
- eSIM activation.
- Network registration after activation.

Check:

- Device eSIM compatibility.
- Whether the device is connected to the internet during setup.
- eSIM activation status.
- Profile installation status.
- Mobile-line status.
- Whether the eSIM profile is enabled.
- Any displayed activation or installation error.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1. Confirm that the device supports eSIM.
2. Connect the device to a reliable Wi-Fi or internet connection.
3. Verify that the eSIM activation information is correct.
4. Attempt the installation through the device's supported eSIM settings.
5. Confirm that the eSIM profile is enabled after installation.
6. Restart the device after successful installation.
7. Check whether the device registers on the mobile network.

Do not ask the customer to repeatedly delete an eSIM profile unless the authorized provider procedure specifically requires it.

## Diagnostic Interpretation

If the QR code cannot be scanned, investigate the activation information or QR-code validity.

If the profile cannot be downloaded, investigate internet connectivity, device compatibility, or activation status.

If the profile installs but there is no network service, investigate line activation, network registration, or provisioning.

If the device does not support eSIM, the customer may require a compatible physical SIM or supported device.

## Human Action

The support agent should:

1. Verify the customer's account and mobile line where required.
2. Confirm device eSIM compatibility.
3. Identify the stage at which activation fails.
4. Check the eSIM activation status.
5. Guide the customer through the approved activation procedure.
6. Verify network registration after activation.
7. Escalate provisioning problems when necessary.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The eSIM profile cannot be provisioned.
- Activation information is invalid or unavailable.
- The profile is installed but the line cannot register.
- The device is compatible but activation repeatedly fails.
- Backend provisioning requires specialist intervention.
- The customer has lost access to a previously active eSIM and requires authorized recovery.

## Important Notes

Do not assume that an eSIM installation problem is caused by the device.

Do not provide or invent activation credentials, QR codes, or provisioning information.

Do not instruct the customer to delete an active eSIM without confirming the consequences and applicable recovery procedure.
